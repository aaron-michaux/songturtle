## I. Exchange

- **Silver Rule**: Don’t do to others what you don’t want done to you.
- **Golden Rule**: Treat others as you’d like to be treated.
- **Platinum Rule**: Treat others as they want to be treated.
- **Diamond Rule**: Think about others the way you want them to think about you.
- **The Blue Ribbon Rule**: Grace is given and received without regard to merit.
- **The Titanium Rule**: If someone finds your boundaries irritating, then you absolutely need those boundaries.
- **The Fool’s Gambit**: The fundamental result of lying is mistrust.
- **The Original Social Contract**: One hundred good deeds may or may not wipe away a single mistake.
- **The Gentleman’s Covenant**: At the heart of every relationship is a tacit conspiracy.
- **The Harmony Rule**: Avoid doing anything that would cause a cat to flatten their ears.

## II. Theatre

- **The Lofty Perch Rule**: The moral high ground is a club for those more equal than others.
- **The Social Signaller**: Moral posturing tells on itself.
- **The Grandstander’s Sleight of Hand**: The idealist does not see trade-offs.
- **The Monkey Court Rule**: We argue like lawyers because we live amongst judges.
- **The Memetic Selection Effect**: Moral entrepreneurs mine a culture for everything of value.
- **Charles Dickens’ Rule**: Imaginary conversations between imaginary people are in-group virtue displays, not a substitute for history.
- **The Moral Community**: The beliefs that bind us are shielded from consequences.
- **The Gadfly in Plato’s Cave**: We choose between the tribe’s protection and recognising its delusions.
- **Darwin’s Dangerous Idea**: Values are instrumental to evolution.
- **The Divine Rule**: The only universal moral truth is that everyone holds their moral truths to be universal.
- **The Painted Tongue Rule**: Know the other side even as you paint your own.

- **Cogito Ergo Sum**: There’s always some construction.
- **The Emotion Chip**: Feelings are the signals of the six-million-year-old self; there is more to you than that.
- **The Feelings Compass Rule**: Irrationality is often merely the rational pursuit of unconscious goals.
- **The Face-Saving Rule**: The easiest person to fool is yourself.
- **The Neuroplastic Straitjacket**: Sometimes we fail because the mind is shaped by the past.
- **The Fundamental Law of Experimental Psychology**: No one knows what’s happening.
- **The Brainiac’s Rule**: Someone who ploughs the field to eat the worms has nothing to learn about growing food.
- **The Inner Chatterbox**: A curious tale in which we are both the player and the mark.

- **The Wanderer’s Return**: What thought has you right now?

- **The Granite Rule**: You are the principal witness to your life.
- **The Instruction for the Examined Life**: The meaning you are after is in the things that you avoid.
- **The Implacable Steward**: Fix the problem, not the blame.
- **The Inner Sovereign**: You can have results or excuses.
- **Epicurus’ Rule**: Distracted appetites are poison.
- **The Pleasure Guidance System**: Desire is a vector, not a reachable end.
- **The Arkenstone Rule**: Put intelligence between yourself and the things you desire.
- **The Ascetic’s Rule**: All things in moderation, including moderation.
- **The Rambler’s Rule**: Thinking rambles; knowing is concise.
- **La Dolce Vita**: Happiness is built on sound habits.
- **The Rule of Sam Gamgee’s Hobbit Hole**: The tea party is done when the cups are washed, dried, and put back in the cupboard where they belong.
- **Tending the Garden at Midnight**: Mania and depression are motivational extremes for choosing the right things to do, and getting them done.
- **The Quiet Gardener’s Rule**: Make compost from the emotional potato peelings of others.
- **The Shaker’s Rule**: A pure heart tends a garden of joy.
- **The Fairytale Ending**: Find your redhead; see her anew every day.
- **The Philosopher’s Stone Rule**: Children are the future; you are the past.
- **Enchanted Clay**: A sunflower turns to the sun or lives in its shadow.
